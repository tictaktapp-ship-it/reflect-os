REFLECT OS — PRIVACY POLICY
Version 1.0 | Effective date: 1 January 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how Reflect OS Ltd ("we", "us", "our") collects,
uses, and protects your personal data when you use Reflect OS ("the Service").

1. DATA CONTROLLER
Reflect OS Ltd is the data controller for personal data processed through
the Service. Contact: privacy@reflect-os.com

2. DATA WE COLLECT
We collect the following categories of personal data:

a) Account data: email address, display name, avatar image
b) Decision data: decision titles, descriptions, outcomes, and related content
   you enter into the Service (encrypted at rest where encryption is enabled)
c) Usage data: login timestamps, feature usage patterns, device type, platform
d) Consent records: your acceptance of these agreements including version,
   timestamp, and platform

3. HOW WE USE YOUR DATA
We use your personal data to:
- Provide, maintain, and improve the Service
- Process your subscription and billing
- Send service-related communications
- Comply with legal obligations
- Respond to your requests and support enquiries

4. LEGAL BASIS FOR PROCESSING
We process your data on the following legal bases:
- Contractual necessity: to provide the Service you have signed up for
- Legitimate interests: to improve the Service and prevent fraud
- Legal obligation: to comply with applicable laws
- Consent: for optional features such as functional cookies

5. DATA RETENTION
We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to
provide the Service. You may request deletion of your account and data at
any time via Settings → Data & Privacy.

6. DATA SECURITY
All data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. We implement
technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk. Decision
content may be end-to-end encrypted within your workspace.

7. DATA SHARING
We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with:
- Service providers who help us operate the Service (e.g. Supabase for
  database and authentication, hosted in the EU)
- Law enforcement where required by applicable law

8. YOUR RIGHTS (GDPR)
If you are based in the EEA or UK, you have the right to:
- Access your personal data
- Rectify inaccurate data
- Erasure ("right to be forgotten")
- Data portability
- Object to processing
- Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise these rights, use Settings → Data & Privacy or contact
privacy@reflect-os.com

9. COOKIES AND TRACKING
We use only functional cookies necessary for the Service to operate, such
as authentication tokens and theme preferences. We do not use advertising
or analytics cookies. You can manage cookie preferences in Settings → Legal & Privacy.

10. CHILDREN
The Service is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly
collect personal data from children.

11. INTERNATIONAL TRANSFERS
Data is hosted within the EU (Supabase eu-west-1 region). Any transfers
outside the EU are subject to appropriate safeguards.

12. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY
We may update this Privacy Policy. We will notify you of material changes
via the app and require re-acceptance where legally required.

13. CONTACT AND COMPLAINTS
For privacy enquiries: privacy@reflect-os.com
You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your national data
protection authority (e.g. ICO in the UK).
